  1. 听力：This farm produces fresh milk and eggs every day. 问出产什么 → 牛奶和鸡蛋
  2. 单项选择：Fresh ___ like carrots and apples ___ sold at the market. → produce; is
  3. 改错：Many new product are shown at the fair. → products
  4. 选词填空（produce / provide / protect）：The sun ___ us with light and heat. → provides
  5. 句型转换：This factory produces toys. → Does … produce …?
